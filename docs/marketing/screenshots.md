# Screenshots and marketing descriptions

Images in `docs/screenshots/` (1920 × 1080, synthetic demo data). Each entry gives a headline, a
one-paragraph description and a short caption for brochures, web pages and social posts.

| # | File | Headline | Description | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01-projects.png` | Your surveys, organised | Every survey is a project that keeps stations, recordings, processing runs, analyses and reports together in one local folder. Open, back up or share a project by copying a folder. | Projects keep everything together, locally. |
| 2 | `02-import.png` | Import that refuses to guess | TXT, CSV, MiniSEED, SAF, GSE2, SEG-2 and SAC. The wizard shows what it parsed, lets you assign N, E and Z explicitly, asks for the sampling rate when the file has none, and validates gaps, clipping and alignment before any analysis. | Explicit component mapping and strict validation. |
| 3 | `03-waveforms.png` | See the data, record every step | Synchronised three-component waveforms with raw/processed overlays, spectra and spectrograms. The processing toolbox is a transparent, ordered chain with live preview and filter-response plots. | A transparent, fully recorded processing chain. |
| 4 | `04-windows.png` | Windows you can trust | Fixed, automatic or hand-drawn windows, STA/LTA transient screening, colour coding by time, and a seismogram player to listen for noise and bookmark it. | Choose, screen and even listen to your windows. |
| 5 | `05-hvtime.png` | Stationarity at a glance | The H/V-versus-time map shows every window's ratio against clock time, so a stable resonance and transient contamination are obvious. | Is the site response stationary? One look. |
| 6 | `06-results.png` | The whole picture of a peak | Mean curve with its dispersion band, single-window ratios, candidate and selected peaks, absolute spectra in dB, T10 limit, and the SESAME box grid, with every criterion reported one by one. | Mean curve, dispersion band and SESAME, one by one. |
